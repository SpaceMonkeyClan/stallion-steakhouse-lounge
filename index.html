The House

Dinner first. Then stay a while.

The Stallion is two rooms under one ranch house roof on the 29 Palms Highway. Up front, a classic steakhouse: a proper dinner, an honest pour, a table you can settle into.

Past the dining room, the lounge runs late with live music. Come for the steak and head home, or come for the band and never see a menu. Most nights, people end up doing both.

On the highway

Minutes from Joshua Tree

Right on Highway 62 in Yucca Valley, on the way to Pioneertown, Joshua Tree and the national park.
